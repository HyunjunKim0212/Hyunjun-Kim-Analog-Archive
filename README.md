## RF & Microwave Circuits Course
This course covers the fundamental principles and practical design concepts of Radio Frequency (RF) and microwave circuits and systems. It focuses on transmission line theory, impedance matching, network analysis using S-parameters, and active/passive microwave device configurations.

### What I learned
*   **Transmission Line Theory & Impedance Matching**
    *   Mastered wave propagation principles on transmission lines, voltage/current distributions, and reflection coefficients.
    *   Utilized the **Smith Chart** to design single-stub, double-stub, and lumped-element **impedance matching networks** for maximum power transfer.
*   **Microwave Network Analysis & S-Parameters**
    *   Analyzed N-port high-frequency networks using **Scattering Parameters (S-parameters)**, impedance matrices ($Z$), and admittance matrices ($Y$).
    *   Evaluated signal flow graphs, insertion loss, return loss, and power divider/coupler dynamics.
*   **Active & Passive RF Circuit Components**
    *   Studied passive components including power dividers (Wilkinson), directional couplers, resonators, and RF filters.
    *   Explored active circuit fundamentals, including high-frequency amplifier design, gain control, stability analysis (K-factor), and noise figure considerations.

## Distributed Parameter Systems Course
This course focuses on the analytical and electromagnetic foundations of distributed parameter systems, where physical dimensions are comparable to or larger than the signal wavelength. It covers Maxwell's equations, plane wave propagation, waveguides, and high-frequency wave-matter interactions.

### What I learned
*   **Electromagnetic Wave Propagation & Maxwell’s Equations**
    *   Applied **Maxwell's equations** in time and frequency domains to analyze dynamic electromagnetic fields.
    *   Studied **uniform plane wave propagation** in various media, including lossless dielectrics, lossy conductors, and dispersive materials.
*   **Waveguide & Cavity Resonator Dynamics**
    *   Analyzed wave guidance in rectangular and cylindrical metallic waveguides, deriving Transverse Electric (**TE**) and Transverse Magnetic (**TM**) mode field distributions.
    *   Calculated cutoff frequencies, phase/group velocities, attenuation constants, and resonant frequencies in cavity resonators.
*   **Boundary Conditions & Distributed Analysis**
    *   Evaluated wave reflections and transmissions at planar dielectric and conductive boundaries under normal and oblique incidence angles.
    *   Bridged lumped circuit element behavior with distributed electromagnetic field concepts for high-frequency system modeling.

## Electronic Circuits Course
This course covers the fundamentals of semiconductor devices, electronic circuit analysis, and single-stage amplifier architectures. It emphasizes the DC biasing, AC small-signal modeling, and transient analysis of diodes, Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs), and Field-Effect Transistors (FETs).

### What I learned
*   **Semiconductor Device Physics & Diode Applications**
    *   Gained an understanding of PN junction physics, forward/reverse bias characteristics, and breakdown mechanisms.
    *   Designed diode applications including rectifiers, clippers, clampers, and voltage regulation circuits.
*   **Transistor Biasing & Small-Signal Modeling**
    *   Analyzed DC operating points (Q-points) and thermal stability for **BJT** and **MOSFET** configurations.
    *   Developed small-signal hybrid- $\pi$ and $T$-models to evaluate AC parameters such as voltage gain, input impedance, and output impedance.
*   **Single-Stage Amplifier Configurations**
    *   Evaluated fundamental single-stage amplifier topologies (Common-Emitter/Source, Common-Base/Gate, Common-Collector/Drain).
    *   Studied frequency response concepts, high-frequency parasitics, and bandwidth limits of single-transistor stages.

## Analog Integrated Circuits Course
This course focuses on the analysis, design, and optimization of analog integrated circuits using CMOS technology. It covers multi-transistor subcircuits, differential amplifiers, precision current mirrors, operational amplifiers, and frequency compensation techniques.

### What I learned
*   **CMOS Current Mirrors & Active Loads**
    *   Designed high-output-impedance current sources and mirrors, including **Cascode**, **Wilson**, and **Widlar** configurations.
    *   Analyzed active loads to maximize open-loop voltage gain in integrated amplifier stages.
*   **Differential Amplifiers & Op-Amp Design**
    *   Mastered differential pair operation, calculating differential-mode gain, common-mode gain, and Common-Mode Rejection Ratio (**CMRR**).
    *   Studied multi-stage operational amplifier (Op-Amp) topologies, output stages, IC biasing, and offset voltage reduction.
*   **Frequency Response & Stability Compensation**
    *   Analyzed high-frequency response using Miller’s theorem and open-circuit time-constant methods.
    *   Applied phase margin criteria and **frequency compensation** techniques (e.g., Pole-Zero / Miller compensation) to ensure closed-loop feedback stability.
